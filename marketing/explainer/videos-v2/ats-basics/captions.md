## LinkedIn (16:9 video + this text)

```
Three resume basics that applicant tracking systems depend on, and that fancy templates often break:

1. Contact details in the body. Many ATSs skip headers, footers and text boxes, and your email goes with them.
2. Standard headings. "Experience", "Education", "Skills", not "Where I've made an impact".
3. Dates on every role, in a readable format, so your experience sorts correctly.

QuickFit CV checks all three on every resume, and every template is built around them.

https://quickfitcv.com

#ats #resume #jobsearch #careertips
```
