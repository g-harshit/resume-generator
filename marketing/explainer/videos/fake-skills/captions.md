## LinkedIn (16:9 video + this text)

```
Quick test: could you explain every line on your resume?

If AI wrote it, maybe not. A lot of AI resume tools will happily add skills, tools and numbers you don't have, because a fuller resume scores better against the job description. It works right up until the interviewer says: "Walk me through a Looker model you built."

I built QuickFit CV to do the opposite:
• It compares your resume with the job and shows exactly which skills are missing
• Skills you don't have stay missing. It never adds them for you
• If you do have one, you add it yourself, in your own words, with where you used it
• Then it rewrites only the lines you choose, in the job's language, keeping your facts

Tailoring should make your real experience easier to see, not invent a new one.

Free to try: https://quickfitcv.com

Recruiters and hiring managers: how often do you catch padded resumes in interviews? I'd love to hear.

#hiring #recruiting #jobsearch #resume #careers
```
