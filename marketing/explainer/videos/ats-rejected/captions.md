50 applications. Zero callbacks?

Most job seekers send one resume everywhere. But every job asks for different skills, and applicant tracking systems filter on them. A resume that matches 5 of a job's 11 skills is often rejected before a recruiter ever opens it.

Tailoring by hand works, but it takes a long time per job, so most people stop doing it.

That's why I built QuickFit CV:
• Paste the job description and see exactly which of its keywords your resume is missing
• Rewrite only the lines you choose, in the job's own words
• Get a clean, ATS-friendly PDF

One rule I didn't compromise on: it never adds a skill, employer, date or number that isn't already yours. If you don't have a skill, it stays missing, and it tells you so.

It's free to try: https://quickfitcv.com

If you're job hunting (or know someone who is), I'd love your feedback.

#jobsearch #resume #ats #careers
