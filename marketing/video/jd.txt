Senior Data Analyst — Northwind Commerce (Bengaluru, Hybrid)

About the role
Northwind Commerce is an online marketplace for home goods. We're looking for a Senior Data Analyst to join our Growth team and help us understand what drives customer retention and repeat purchases.

What you'll do
- Own dashboards and reporting for the Growth team in Tableau or Looker.
- Write complex SQL to analyse orders, customer behaviour and marketing performance.
- Design and analyse A/B tests with product managers and share clear recommendations.
- Build customer segmentation and cohort analyses to guide retention campaigns.
- Automate recurring reports with Python.
- Present findings to senior stakeholders and leadership.

What we're looking for
- 3+ years of experience as a data analyst, ideally in e-commerce or retail.
- Strong SQL and Python (pandas).
- Experience with Tableau or Looker.
- Solid grasp of statistics and experimentation (A/B testing).
- Excellent stakeholder communication.

Nice to have
- dbt and BigQuery.
- Cohort and retention analysis.
- Experience with customer lifetime value (CLV) modelling.
